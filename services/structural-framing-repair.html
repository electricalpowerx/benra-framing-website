Is a bouncy floor always serious?
Not always, but it's always worth an assessment â€” sometimes it's a simple joist reinforcement, other times it points to a bigger issue like an undersized beam. We'll tell you honestly which it is.
Do you need an engineer for every repair?
No â€” many repairs (sistering a joist, replacing a rotted post) don't require new engineering. We bring one in specifically when a fix changes how loads are carried.

Often yes, especially from below in a basement or crawlspace. We'll explain what access is realistically needed for your specific repair.
