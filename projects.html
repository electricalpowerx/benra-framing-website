Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical framing project take?
It varies widely by scope — a deck might take 3–5 days, a small addition 2–3 weeks, and a full new-home framing package 3–4 weeks. We give you a realistic schedule as part of every written quote.
Do you provide references from past clients?
Yes — ask when you call and we'll connect you with recent clients happy to talk about their experience.
Do you handle projects of all sizes?
Yes — from a small repair to a full new-home framing package or a multi-unit commercial build, we scale the crew to the project.
