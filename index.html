Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get a quote?
Most written quotes are turned around within 24–48 hours of a site visit or plan review.
Do you handle both residential and commercial framing?
Yes — new-home and renovation framing for homeowners, plus multi-family and commercial tenant-improvement framing for builders and GCs.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Benra Framing and Renovations Limited is a licensed carpentry contractor in British Columbia, carrying comprehensive liability insurance and WorkSafeBC (WCB) registration.
Do you pull permits for structural work?
Yes — permits and, where required, engineering are part of how we handle load-bearing changes, additions, secondary suites and new construction.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Surrey and serve the entire Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley — see our full service area list.
