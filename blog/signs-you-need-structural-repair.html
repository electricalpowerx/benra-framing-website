7 Signs Your Home Needs Structural Framing Repair

Every home settles a little over time. The signs below go beyond normal settling and are worth a proper structural assessment â€” here's what we tell every homeowner not to ignore.

1. A visibly sagging or bouncy floor

A floor that visibly dips in the middle of a room, or feels bouncy when you walk across it, usually points to an undersized or damaged joist or beam.

2. Doors and windows that suddenly stick

A door or window that used to close fine and now sticks or won't latch can mean the frame around it has shifted â€” sometimes cosmetic, sometimes a sign of structural movement.

3. Diagonal cracks above doors and windows

Diagonal drywall cracks radiating from the corners of door and window openings often indicate a header that's undersized or a foundation that's settled unevenly.

4. A sloping floor

A floor that noticeably slopes toward one side of a room (check with a level, not just by feel) can indicate a failing support post, beam or foundation issue underneath.

5. Visible rot or soft wood

Soft, spongy wood you can press a screwdriver into â€” especially around decks, ledger boards, or where framing meets grade â€” has lost structural integrity and needs assessment.

6. Gaps opening up between walls and ceilings

New or widening gaps where a wall meets the ceiling or floor can indicate settling or a structural member giving way somewhere in the load path.

7. A support post that's visibly leaning or crushed

Basement or crawlspace support posts that lean, or show crushing at the top or bottom, are a clear sign the load isn't being carried the way it should be.
