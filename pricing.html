HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the written quote really free?
Yes. Every quote is free and provided in writing, typically within 24–48 hours of a plan review or site visit — with no obligation to book.
What does a small repair call-out cost?
Small repair visits start at a $175 flat assessment rate, which is credited toward the job if you move forward with the repair.
What's included in new home framing pricing?
Our new home framing starts around $32 per square foot for a standard single-family structure, including labour, standard framing lumber and site cleanup — engineered lumber and complex rooflines are quoted separately.
Does deck pricing include the decking material?
Our deck pricing covers the structure — footings, posts, beams and joists. Decking surface material (wood, composite, etc.) is quoted separately based on what you choose.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. You receive a clear, written quote before any work begins. Any change to scope is discussed and approved by you before we proceed.
