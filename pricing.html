Quoting FAQ

Do you publish a price list?
No. Every framing, deck, renovation or commercial job is quoted in writing after we see the plans or the site. Photos through the form are a good start.
Are quotes free?
Yes. Use the contact form. A phone number is coming soon. Most written quotes are back within 24–48 hours of a plan review or site visit.
What affects a framing quote?
Scope, access, engineering, permits, material grades and how new work ties into the existing structure. We explain those factors in the written quote — not as a public rate card.
Does a deck quote include the decking surface?
Structure (footings, posts, beams and joists) and decking surface are scoped clearly on the written quote so you know what is included before we start.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. You receive a clear, written quote before any work begins. Any change to scope is discussed and approved by you before we proceed.
